# Tank core, T1 (2026-09-23)

The boss's swings on a tank: the table (miss, dodge, parry, block, crit, crushing) against a boss
of level 60–63, and the mitigation (armor, damage-taken modifiers, crit ×2, crush ×1.5, then
block value). Also full blocks, `D_pre` for rage, parry haste, and crit reduction. It adds proc
triggers (`dodge`, `parry`, `meleeTaken`, `critTaken`), defensive aura mods, block charges,
`plan.rage.fromDamageTaken`, and `SimResult.tank`. D24 placeholders for base health and base
parry and block. Commits `4980557`..`a78aad7` on `main`, rebased onto `9f6ca68`.

Fury and Arms are bit-identical to before. Protection moves only from the base-health
placeholder: 216.990 → 217.042 TPS.
