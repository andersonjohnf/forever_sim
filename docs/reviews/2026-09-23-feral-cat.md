# Feral Cat, B2 (2026-09-23)

The first druid spec to ship. The cat's abilities and rotation (Shred, Claw, Rake, Rip, Ferocious
Bite, Tiger's Fury, Berserk, Faerie Fire), tuned under D23, then offered in the switcher. Built on
the druid foundation (B1), rebased onto main `5166211`: `cbb501b`..`3f79869`.

The tuned defaults beat the doc's first priority by **+20.39 DPS (+3.73%, 547.05 → 567.44, 95% CI
+20.31 to +20.47)** on a fresh seed; the review round's end-of-fight Bite adds +0.82 (+0.15%). The
Cat golden is 567.60. Fury, Arms, Protection and the paladins are byte-identical to main.
